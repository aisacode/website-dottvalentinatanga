## Users

- **Primary:** adults in Sardinia (Cagliari and Sassari areas) living with acute or chronic pain, often after a first drug-based approach that did not help. They arrive wanting to understand whether pain therapy is for them, what a visit involves, and where and how to be seen. Many are searching on a phone.
- **Secondary (assumed, not confirmed):** family members searching on a patient's behalf, and GPs or specialists who want to check her training, treatments, and locations before referring.

## Product Purpose

A personal professional website for **Dott.ssa Valentina Tanga**, specialist in Anestesia, Rianimazione e Terapia del Dolore (pain therapist and anaesthesiologist). The site is primarily **informational**: it explains who she is, which conditions she treats, which treatments she uses, and where she practises. It does not take bookings itself; every booking action sends the visitor to her MioDottore profile. Success is a visitor who understands pain therapy well enough to decide to be seen, and who then reaches the MioDottore profile or the phone number without friction.

## Positioning

- An **algologist with hospital pain-centre training** (Sassari school; HUB pain centre in Alessandria, pain clinics in Chivasso, Mater Olbia; currently at the SC Terapia del Dolore, PO Businco, Cagliari) who also sees patients privately in Cagliari and Sassari and online.
- Her stance, from her own brochure: pain that resists treatment is the most important symptom of disease and must be investigated and treated, not endured. Resignation amplifies pain and raises the risk of it becoming chronic. A pain specialist widens the treatment options but above all gets to a correct diagnosis of the cause.
- Breadth across pharmacological, injection, regenerative, and neuromodulation techniques, applied to benign and oncological pain alike.

## Product Principles

1. **Explain before you ask.** The visitor's job is understanding; the booking link is the natural consequence, not the opening move.
2. **One exit, clearly marked.** Every path to an appointment ends at the MioDottore profile or the phone number. The site never pretends to book.
3. **Her words, her credentials.** Copy comes from her brochure and her profile. No invented testimonials, statistics, or outcome promises, and nothing borrowed from Villa Esperia's identity.
4. **Serious and reassuring, never alarming or salesy.** The subject is suffering; the tone treats it with respect and offers a path, in the register of a specialist speaking plainly.
5. **Phone-first, low-friction.** Patients in pain read on a phone. Addresses, numbers, and the booking link must be reachable in one tap from anywhere.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Audience includes older adults and people in pain who may have limited concentration and dexterity. Large readable Italian text, high contrast, generous tap targets, and meaning never carried by colour alone. Target WCAG 2.2 AA as a working standard (assumed; not an explicit client requirement).
